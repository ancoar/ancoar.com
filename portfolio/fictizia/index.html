Microinteraction to enhance UX and increase conversions

The process of requesting more information about a training plan is the primary action on the product detail page, making it crucial to optimize every aspect of it. To achieve this, I designed a user-friendly and intuitive form that provides maximum feedback at every step.

Additionally, I created, animated, and implemented (SVG + CSS + JS) a microinteraction to enhance the UX of this critical request process through storytelling. A paper airplane takes off from the submit button, flying across the screen until it "reaches Fictizia" in the confirmation dialog, where the user receives information about the final status of their request.

The microinteraction of the paper airplane: Taking the utmost care of the UX in the most important user flow of the product detail page.
