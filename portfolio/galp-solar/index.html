GALP SOLAR is one of the leading Iberian operators of photovoltaic self-consumption with more than 6,000 customers in Spain and Portugal.

CLIENT

Galp Solar

YEAR

2021

DISCIPLINES

UI Design, Brand Design

CHALLENGE

EI Energía Independiente was a startup dedicated to photovoltaic solar self-consumption backed by the oil company Galp. After a change of strategy, in 2021 it was decided that the company would live entirely under the umbrella of the Galp brand, thus becoming Galp Solar. This project focused on the redesign of the company's corporate site galpsolar.com, adopting and applying a visual language in line with Galp's brand (still without a design system) to make it cohesive and be recognized as the solar energy division of the large Portuguese oil company.
